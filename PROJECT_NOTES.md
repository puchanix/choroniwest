# Design / product notes

## Product intent

The application should feel like an invitation to make work, not like institutional paperwork.

Key principles:

- Short, clear answers are explicitly welcomed.
- Applicants are told they are artists, not grant writers.
- AI assistance is allowed, provided the final application accurately reflects the applicant's own ideas and voice.
- Sections are deliberately small and navigable in any order.
- Progress is visible without turning the process into a gamified checklist.
- No matching funds are required.
- A grant may cover up to 100% of project costs, but proposals may be funded in full or in part.
- No database or account creation is required.

## Visual direction

The design deliberately avoids a corporate/SaaS aesthetic. It uses an editorial arts-foundation vocabulary: oversized serif typography, poster-like color blocks, visible rules, off-register decorative forms, paper texture, and restrained interaction.
